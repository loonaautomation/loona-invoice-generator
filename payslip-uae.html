Payslip in United Arab Emirates

A AED 240,000 annual salary in United Arab Emirates nets AED 240,000 a year after tax and the contributions this calculator models. The worked payslip below shows every line — and the calculator works out any salary.

In short: at AED 240,000 a year, the income tax is AED 0, the social contributions modelled are AED 0, and the net pay is AED 240,000 a year (AED 20,000 a month).

There is no personal income tax on employment salaries in the UAE, at federal or emirate level, in any year — the calculator returns gross as net. The 9% corporate tax (on profits over AED 375,000) does not apply to individual employment income. End-of-service gratuity is a separate entitlement, not a deduction.

What a payslip in United Arab Emirates usually shows

  • The employee’s name, employee number and job title
  • The pay period and the pay date
  • Basic salary plus allowances (housing, transport, other)
  • Any deductions (advances, loans, or other agreed deductions)
  • Net pay, usually paid by bank transfer through the WPS
  • End-of-service accrual is tracked separately, not deducted from pay

Frequently asked questions

Is there income tax on a UAE salary?

No — the UAE levies no personal income tax on employment salaries. The payslip below shows gross pay equal to net pay. The 9% corporate tax does not apply to individual employment income.

What is on a UAE payslip?

Basic salary and allowances, any agreed deductions such as advances or loans, and the net pay. There is no tax or social security line for most expatriate employees.

What about end-of-service benefits?

Expatriate employees are entitled to an end-of-service gratuity under UAE labour law. It is an entitlement paid when employment ends, not a deduction from monthly pay — work it out with the Loona UAE gratuity calculator.
