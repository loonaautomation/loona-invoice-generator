Invoice in Spain

The standard VAT rate in Spain is 21%. This guide shows what an invoice in Spain must include, works an example through, and opens the free Loona invoice generator with the rate and currency already set.

In short: the standard VAT rate in Spain is 21%, charged in EUR.

What must an invoice in Spain show?

  • A unique, sequential invoice number
  • The date the invoice is issued
  • Your full business name and address, and your VAT identification number
  • Your client’s full name and address (and their VAT ID for cross-border B2B sales)
  • A description of the goods or services, with quantity and unit price
  • The VAT rate applied, the VAT amount and the total due
  • The reverse-charge note where the reverse charge applies, or the exemption reference where an exemption is used
  • Your NIF (tax identification number) and your customer’s NIF
  • The invoice number, in an unbroken series
  • The VAT rate per line (21%, 10% or 4%) and the VAT amount

Notes for Spain

  • Spain’s standard VAT rate (IVA) is 21%. Reduced rates of 10% and 4% apply to food, transport, hotels, books and some other goods and services.
  • Spain’s immediate supply of information system (SII) requires larger businesses to report invoice details to the tax office within a few days.
  • A simplified invoice (factura simplificada) may be used for small amounts instead of a full invoice.

Source: standard VAT rate taken from the Loona Salary & Tax Calculators VAT pages, checked 9 November 2026. This guide is general information, not tax advice — confirm anything unusual with your accountant.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard VAT rate in Spain?

The Spanish standard VAT rate (IVA) is 21%. Reduced rates of 10% and 4% apply to food, transport, hotels, books and some other goods. This page’s worked example uses the 21% standard rate.

What is a factura simplificada?

It is Spain’s simplified invoice for small amounts: it needs fewer details than a full invoice (no customer name or address is required). Use a full invoice for business customers who need to reclaim VAT.
