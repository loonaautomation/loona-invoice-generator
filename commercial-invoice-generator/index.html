Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a commercial invoice and a proforma invoice?

A proforma is issued before the sale ships — it is a pre-sale document for confirming the deal or arranging payment. A commercial invoice accompanies the actual shipment and is the document customs uses to assess duties, so it carries HS codes, origin and incoterms.

What is an HS code?

The Harmonized System code is the international tariff classification for goods (usually 6 digits, sometimes extended nationally). Customs uses it to set duty rates. Look your product up in your country's tariff schedule or ask your freight forwarder.
