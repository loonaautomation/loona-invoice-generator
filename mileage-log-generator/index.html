Frequently asked questions

How is mileage reimbursement calculated?

Miles for a trip = odometer end − odometer start. Reimbursement = total miles × your rate per mile. Example: trips of 50 and 75.5 miles at 0.50 per mile give 125.5 miles and 62.75 due.

Which rate per mile should I use?

Use your employer's rate, or your country's official mileage rate if you claim it on taxes — those official rates change over time and differ by country, so enter the rate that applies to you.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up, no account and no watermark.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser on your device. Your document data is never uploaded to a server.

How do I download a PDF?

Click Print / Download PDF and choose Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog. That produces a clean A4 PDF.
