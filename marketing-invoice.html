Invoice for Marketing Agency

Invoicing for marketing agency is straightforward once you know what belongs on the invoice. This guide lists the usual line items, the payment terms that work in this trade, and the tax notes — with a worked example at a clearly-labelled example rate.

What to put on the invoice

  • A monthly retainer, with the services included and the period covered
  • A campaign, with the media spend shown separately from your fee
  • Design or content work, by item or by hour
  • Ad spend paid on the client’s behalf, shown separately from your fee

Payment terms

Agencies usually invoice retainers monthly in advance, and project work per milestone or on completion. Net 30 is common. Media spend is often invoiced as it is incurred, with your fee shown as a separate line.

Tax notes

Agency services are normally taxed at the standard rate. Ad spend bought on the client’s behalf is usually recharged at cost and carries tax in the same way as your fee — confirm the treatment in your country. Frequently asked questions

How should I invoice a monthly retainer?

One line for the retainer fee and the month it covers, plus separate lines for any work above the retainer and any media spend. The client should be able to see what the retainer covered.

Should ad spend be a separate line?

Yes — show the media spend separately from your agency fee. Clients need to see what went to the platform and what went to you.

Do I charge tax on ad spend I recharge?

Usually, yes — recharged ad spend is part of your taxable supply in most countries. Confirm the treatment in your country, and keep the platform’s own invoices as your records.
