Frequently asked questions

How is the amount due on an expense report calculated?

Amount due = total expenses − any advance already received. Example: 320 of expenses with a 100 advance leaves 220 due to the claimant.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up, no account and no watermark.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser on your device. Your document data is never uploaded to a server.

How do I download a PDF?

Click Print / Download PDF and choose Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog. That produces a clean A4 PDF.
