Invoice in Norway

The standard VAT rate in Norway is 25%. This guide shows what an invoice in Norway must include, works an example through, and opens the free Loona invoice generator with the rate and currency already set.

In short: the standard VAT rate in Norway is 25%, charged in NOK.

What must an invoice in Norway show?

  • Your organisasjonsnummer (organisation number) and MVA registration
  • A sequential invoice number and the date of issue
  • Your business name and address, and your customer’s name and address
  • A description of the goods or services, with quantity and unit price
  • The MVA rate (25%), the MVA amount in NOK and the total due
  • The VAT amount in NOK, even when the invoice is issued in another currency

Notes for Norway

  • Norway’s standard VAT rate (MVA) is 25%. Reduced rates of 15% (food) and 12% (passenger transport, hotels, culture) apply.
  • Norway is not in the EU, so the EU reverse charge does not apply — but Norwegian VAT rules for cross-border services are similar in practice, and you must be registered in the Merverdiavgiftsregisteret to charge MVA.
  • Registration is compulsory once turnover passes NOK 50,000 in 12 months.

Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT rate in Norway?

The Norwegian standard VAT rate (MVA) is 25%. Reduced rates of 15% (food) and 12% (passenger transport, hotels and culture) apply. This page’s worked example uses the 25% standard rate.

Is Norway covered by the EU reverse charge?

No — Norway is not an EU member, so the EU reverse-charge rules do not apply as such. Cross-border services follow Norwegian VAT rules instead, so confirm the treatment with your accountant for sales to or from Norway.
