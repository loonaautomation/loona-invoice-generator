Payslip in Pakistan

A PKR 2,400,000 annual salary in Pakistan nets PKR 2,238,000 a year after tax and the contributions this calculator models. The worked payslip below shows every line — and the calculator works out any salary.

In short: at PKR 2,400,000 a year, the income tax is PKR 162,000, the social contributions modelled are PKR 0, and the net pay is PKR 2,238,000 a year (PKR 186,500 a month).

Salaried individuals only. Tax year 2026 = TY2026 (Jul 2025–Jun 2026, Finance Act 2025) slabs; a 10% surcharge applies above PKR 10 million. The newer Finance Act 2026 slabs (TY2027, Jul 2026–Jun 2027) are a different year — the site’s Pakistan Salary Tax Calculator covers those as its default.

What a payslip in Pakistan usually shows

  • The employee’s name, CNIC and employee code
  • The pay period (month) and the days worked
  • Gross salary and allowances
  • Income tax deducted at source, and EOBI and provident fund deductions where they apply
  • Net pay, and the tax deducted so far this tax year

Frequently asked questions

What is on a Pakistani payslip?

The employee’s name, CNIC and code, the pay month, gross salary and allowances, income tax deducted at source, EOBI and provident fund deductions where they apply, and the net pay.

Which tax year do these figures use?

Tax year 2026 (July 2025 to June 2026, Finance Act 2025): 0% to PKR 600,000, then 1%, 11%, 23%, 30% and 35%, with a 10% surcharge above PKR 10 million. The newer Finance Act 2026 slabs for TY2027 are different — do not mix the two years.

How do I make a payslip for a Pakistani employee?

Use the free Loona pay stub generator below for the layout, and the calculator on this page for the income-tax figure. Add EOBI and provident fund deductions from the employee’s own rates.
